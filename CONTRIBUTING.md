# Contributing

The useful entries here are the ones written by someone who actually
integrated the source. That is what to send.

## What belongs

A source of European electricity market data, with:

- **what it holds** — which series, at what resolution, how far back
- **how you get access** — free or not, key or not, and how long that takes
- **what surprises you** — the thing that cost you an afternoon

An entry without the third part is a bookmark, not an entry.

## What does not belong

- Commercial data vendors with no free tier
- Links with no description
- Anything you have not used yourself

## Corrections

These are the most valuable contributions, because this kind of list rots:
endpoints move, registration steps change, APIs get rebuilt. If something here
is wrong, say so in an issue — no need to write the fix yourself.

## Format

One bullet per source. Bold name as a link, then what it is, then `*Access:*`
and `*Traps:*` lines where they apply. Keep it to a few lines; the long version
belongs in your own write-up, which you are welcome to link.
